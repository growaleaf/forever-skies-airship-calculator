Airship Weight & Power Calculator

Your airship stops climbing and nobody onboard can tell you why: too much weight, or not enough power. This separates the two and tells you which upgrade actually fixes it — a cheaper balloon expansion, a whole new balloon core, or another generator.

Lift budget

Your airship starts at 500 kg of lift. Every additional balloon core adds another 500 kg; every balloon expansion you've bought adds 400 kg. Enter what you've built, then your current ship weight off the Cargo/Weight panel.

Power budget

Forever Skies doesn't publish a fixed fuel-burn rate per generator — burn scales with how much total power your devices are drawing, not a flat number per generator tier. So this reads the two numbers straight off your own Power panel rather than guessing at them.

Why generator wattage isn't in the calculator above. forever-skies-database.fandom.com lists Basic/Advanced/Next-Gen Power Generators at 100, 250 and 400 E/m, but every other source this build checked (Steam community guides, gamepressure.com, techraptor.net) either didn't cover it or was unreachable this session — so these three numbers have exactly one source, not the two this lane requires before treating a number as confirmed. They're shown here only as a reference, not built into the arithmetic. Read your own Fabricator tooltip before relying on them.

Turbines are a separate system, deliberately left out. Turbines set your maximum altitude, not your lift — ship weight has no effect on the altitude ceiling, only on whether you can take off and hover at all. The specific turbine-to-altitude numbers conflicted across the sources this build checked (one Steam discussion gave +20 m per turbine with a 230 masl floor, another gave +20/+30 m with a 220 masl floor), so no turbine numbers are published on this page. Held for a later session alongside the interactive location map.
